# Durable campaign systems

28 October 2026. Godot 4.7.2.stable.official.ed1daf0bf. Continues [campaign foundation](campaign-foundation.md). This batch implements the remaining core data/rule boundaries before native management integration. **F5 still opens the accepted sandbox.** No campaign menu, Discovered view, recovery dialog or lifecycle autosave is exposed yet.

## Changes

`persistence/campaign_session.gd` is the local coordinator. It executes commands on a validated private controller, writes a pending journal containing both proposed files, commits run/profile, then closes the journal before publishing success. Generations and transaction IDs guard replay; recovery completes interrupted commits, rejects conflicting/stale writers and preserves damaged files. The closed journal retains the latest payload for backup repair. Autosaving a live match keeps its instance identity. Failed persistence blocks additional commands until reconciliation. No file locks/multiple simultaneous writers are supported; stale sessions are rejected rather than silently replacing newer progress.

`persistence/profile.gd` records only acquired card versions, their first UTC discovery date/run identities, and factual run summaries. Offers, opponents and browsing grant nothing. Repeated projection/recovery cannot increment a discovery twice. Records contain content/balance labels, actual non-shootout goal difference and goals by historical event card ID; academy/own goals cannot become player records. Fresh runs retain information only and start with $8m/16 ordinary players. Terminal heads remain recoverable in the run/journal files, are absent from Continue previews, and can be replaced by a fresh run. No public summary/abandon confirmation screen or final file-pruning flow is claimed.

`run/records.gd` captures the exact eleven in a saved valid lineup, including its formation, integer slot-adjusted base ratings and exact total/11. Bench strength, temporary bonuses, suspended starters and academy cover cannot inflate it. This follows GDD §14's achieved lineup definition; it does not silently optimize hypothetical XIs. Completed histories sort by championship, wins, fewer defeats, peak XI and non-shootout goal difference, with stable run-ID ties.

`match/coaching.gd` implements all six conditional +3 principles on real ground passes/crosses, standing tackles, direct free kicks/immediate corner headers, controlled recovery runs and substitute physical attributes. Their bonus joins traits inside the existing +6 cap, before role/fatigue and the 120 ceiling. Nothing consumes extra RNG or mutates card ratings. Existing recovery ticks supply the two-active-second window; no new timer path. Windup shots retain their actual restart context. Match snapshots now include principles; standalone schema-1 snapshots restore with no principles, and schema 2 validates both teams' choices.

`run/availability.gd` derives accumulated yellows/red bans from completed match event receipts. Three yellows or a red issues one ban and resets the counter; a second-yellow red cannot stack. The next completed attempt serves it, win or loss. Visiting preparation or resuming a partial match does not. Preparation excludes banned people from the entire match squad, including substitution candidates. Missing XI/keeper coverage uses stable anonymous 48-OVR definitions only in the match plan. They cannot become ownership, discovery, acquisition candidates or peak records. Owned plans and actual available match plans are persisted separately. Incoming native substitutes also now reset the outgoing player's match yellow count.

Run state **schema 3** validates achieved XI, event-derived disciplinary ledgers, committed available plans, served bans, immutable content/academy definitions and coaching, in addition to the original finances/identity/RNG checks. Earlier foundation run schemas 1/2 are incompatible and retained for recovery rather than silently reinterpreted. Profile/journal/envelope schemas are 1. No cross-content/build migration is claimed.
